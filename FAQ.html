Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: Who can use PiLord?
PiLord is designed for users aged 6 years and older.
Children should use the app with appropriate parent or guardian involvement, consent, or supervision as required by applicable law.
Q2: Do I need an account or email to play?
PiLord currently works as a guest-first app.
Players can use the app without creating an account or entering an email address.
Q3: What is the difference between PiLord Lite and PiLord?
PiLord Lite is the free version and may include limited content or limited features.
PiLord is the full paid version distributed through supported app stores. The current app model has no recurring subscription and no in-app advertising.
Q4: What data is saved?
Gameplay progress, settings, coins, and unlocked content are stored locally on the device.
We may collect limited pseudonymous usage analytics, such as app information and gameplay activity, to improve the app.
